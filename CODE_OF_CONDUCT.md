# Code Of Conduct

Megavibe is a builder community. The goal is to make ambitious work easier to start, discuss, test, and ship.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and respectful.
- Critique ideas and implementation details, not people.
- Share enough context for others to understand your work.
- Credit contributors and sources.
- Keep discussions useful for future readers.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Sharing private information without permission
- Spam or repeated low-effort promotion
- Misrepresenting work, results, ownership, or sources

## Reporting

Open a private maintainer channel or contact the repository owner if something needs moderation.
